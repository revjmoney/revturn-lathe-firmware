R9 INLINE MOTOR MOUNT - EXTENDED SINGLE-SPLIT RAIL COLLARS

Print ONE inline_motor_mount_EXTENDED_SPLIT_CLAMPS_PRINT.stl.

Both integral rail supports now run from 0.157" beyond the outside
tower face all the way to the inside motor plate at 1.250".
Each encloses its rail and has an approximately 0.024" radial slit.
Two vertical pinch screws per rail tighten the outer jaws. No separate caps.
The original four side M4 holes, 0.250" flange, 0.250" motor plate,
four corner gussets and rounded exposed edges remain.

Fit, in inches:
- Rail diameter 0.393"; centre spacing 1.496".
- Nominal bore diameter 0.397" (0.004" diametral clearance).
- Collar length 1.093"; actual rail engagement 1.024" with 1.181" rails.
- Keep both rails approximately 1.181" beyond tower; shorten the long
  rail to match the short one. Rail tips end 0.069" before the plate.
- Inside motor plate face 1.250" beyond tower; motor front face 1.500".
- Motor body length approximately 1.750".
- Coupler length assumed 0.984"; envelope diameter 0.787".
- Central opening 0.945" diameter, at least 0.079" radial clearance.
- Coupler starts 0.043" beyond tower; screw projection nominal 0.516".
- Shaft engagement 0.472" each; shaft end gap 0.039".

Hardware:
- Four M3 pinch screws, nominal M3 x 20 mm, four M3 nuts, no washers
  in the recessed heads. Hex pockets approximately 0.224" across flats
  and 0.098" deep. Check actual nut fit and bolt length.
- Four original M4 side screws, nominal M4 x 16 mm with thin washers.
  Avoid bottoming in the original tower threads.
- Four M3 motor screws, nominal M3 x 10 mm. Check allowed motor thread depth.
Existing metric fastener sizes are retained for compatibility.

Assembly:
1. Clean support material out of bores, nut pockets, head recesses and
   the full clamp slits. Ensure the jaws are free before inserting rails.
2. Insert the four captive nuts from underneath and start pinch screws
   loosely. Slide the mount onto the rail ends toward the tower.
3. Seat the side flange against the tower and start the four M4 screws.
4. Fit motor/coupler, check smooth screw rotation, then tighten side
   screws evenly. Tighten pinch screws gradually and only enough to grip.
   Do not crush the printed jaws or bottom the slit.
5. Preserve original fixed-end bearings/collars for leadscrew thrust.
Coupling setscrews and pinch heads are accessible from above.

Shaft projection remains unmeasured: modeled at 0.945" from the motor
front. Actual coupler length and bore depths are also unconfirmed.
Check these before printing or setting screw length. The 1.250" plate
gap is fixed by the four side attachment holes.

Printing:
PRINT STL is oriented motor mounting face down. Use build-plate supports.
The verification uses the saved Ender profile at approximately 0.012"
layers, five perimeters and 35% infill. See slice-verification.json.
Deburr locating faces; do not enlarge the bores unnecessarily.

Files:
- PRINT STL: single printable bracket.
- Main STEP/FCStd and assembly STL: original R9 coordinates.
- inline_side_tie_assembly STEP/FCStd: bracket plus reference hardware
  and original saddle/slide, for inspection only.
- build_split_mount.py, parameters.json, helpers and frozen R9 source:

Geometric and slicer checks cannot establish printed clamp compliance,
physical hardware fit, cutting stiffness or durability. This remains
a prototype requiring a gentle physical fit and clamp test.
